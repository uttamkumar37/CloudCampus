# CloudCampus Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

CloudCampus processes school, student, parent, staff, academic, fee, attendance, communication, audit, and support data to provide secure school operations for demos, controlled pilots, and customer environments.

CloudCampus does not sell student, parent, staff, or school operational data. Data is used to provide the platform, authenticate users, enforce tenant and role isolation, support school workflows, maintain backups, monitor reliability, investigate incidents, and improve the service.

Customer schools are responsible for the accuracy, permissions, notices, and lawful basis for data they enter into CloudCampus. CloudCampus uses access controls, password hashing, audit logs, rate limits, encrypted backups, monitoring, and operational security practices to protect customer data.

Schools may request export, correction, or deletion of customer data through their CloudCampus contact or support channel. Backup copies may retain deleted data for a limited recovery window before normal expiry.

Privacy questions and school-pilot privacy reviews can be sent to privacy@cloudcampus.io. This pilot-ready policy should be reviewed by counsel before broad commercial rollout.
